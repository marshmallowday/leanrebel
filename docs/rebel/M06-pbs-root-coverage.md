## Explicit hypotheses and semantic review

The construction accepts a FinDist of legal original histories. Calling it a PBS
requires instantiating that law with PublicBelief.law; the positive control uses
finiteBudgetControlBelief, a genuine factual posterior from canonical stopped
play. No tuple-of-marginals approximation or independence assumption is used.
The original protocol's action types and transition law are retained. The extra
initial chance draw consumes exactly one fuel unit and is never a player move.
Full original history finiteness, not finite physical states alone, yields the
strict rank and the derived rooted history enumeration. The actual CFR theorem
uses two players, finite original actions and histories, a legal original
fallback, a zero-sum payoff on every history and explicit absolute payoff bounds.
The full-AOH construction supplies the clock and perfect recall. Synthetic
information states have the original legal menus; the administrative menu is a
singleton. Neither a world history nor a joint hidden root is a policy argument.

The original-to-root behavioral correspondence is an equality of complete
projected runner laws at every finite fuel, not merely one expectation. The
coordinatewise lift commutes with canonical unilateral strategy replacement.
It does not yet convert an arbitrary NEW rooted CFR policy into an original
policy. In particular, the native Nash theorem must not be reported as a Nash
or safe-re-solving theorem in the original continuation game without that step.
This source is an abstract real-valued reference, not an executable refinement.
