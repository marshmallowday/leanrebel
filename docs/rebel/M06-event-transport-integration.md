## Mathematical slice and limitations

The selected query uses actual tagged canonical execution, not independent
resampling. The conditional native-tail bound is B_T / (actual event mass *
positive threshold). The unconditional event-and-bad-native-gap bound is
B_T / threshold, including impossible events without inventing a posterior.
Its public specialization reads only the resulting canonical public trace.
The native gap retains ORIGINAL compatible full joint type kernels and the
SAME computed average comparison opponent, even though actual execution may
use any fixed legal opponent. No policy learns the retained private seed.

The live example runs the actual full-AOH budget-1/8 solver and bounds the
joint public-and-error-at-least-1/4 probability by 1/2. Sharp diagonal controls
prove conditional tail one and event-weighted tail one half, and refute dropping
the event-mass denominator. Finite-law controls are not represented as actual
CFR-generated gap tables. All existing public-posterior, hidden-selection,
correlated-query and impossible-observation controls remain.

The companion executed opponent/model source charge remains a genuine derived
law bound, not a claim that arbitrary changing opponents make it small. Merely
combining these modules does not identify independently re-solved PBSs or
transfer native gaps to changed posterior kernels/opponents. Quantitative small
support/first-exit rates, changed-PBS native and late rates, recursive safety and
CarriedResolveStepBounds remain open. SEARCH-FRONTIER, SEARCH-CFRD, SEARCH-ERROR
and SAFE-THEOREM3 retain their original pending statuses. M06 and Theorem 3 are
not complete; M07 is not started.
