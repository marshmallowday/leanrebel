## Ordered proof slices

1. Joint history laws supported on a public-history fiber; positive-mass public
   conditioning, explicit impossible observations, and distinct unnormalized
   reach weights. Preserve correlated chance and incompatible private states.
2. PBS-rooted continuation using the canonical Protocol execution. Prove
   public disintegration and that Bayes updates commute with execution, then
   transport arbitrary outcome observables and cumulative/future rewards.
   Separate real terminal histories from depth-limited cut leaves.
3. Information-local prescriptions and strategy restriction/extension. Use
   all realizable information states, including off-policy histories, not
   just the support of a reference policy. State the legal-menu nonemptiness
   premise explicitly wherever an extension needs a fallback choice.
   Prove coordinatewise deviations, not merely a fixed-profile outcome match.
4. Compact encoding with a retained chance/compatibility factor and explicit
   normalization. Prove its connection to the reference joint semantics.
   Marginal products are not a general encoding. Check card exclusion,
   correlated types, off-path choices, and fully public degeneration.
5. Integrate all modules into build/lint/transitive axiom consumers; inspect
   target-SHA CI and update coverage/STATUS with exact declarations,
   assumptions, semantic review and immutable evidence links.

## Semantic boundaries to audit

* The paper's Section 4 referee argument refers to the Section 3 convention
  that policies are common knowledge, while private random outcomes are not.
  Announcing prescriptions and sampling their actions are distinct operations.
  A theorem about public-history-indexed prescriptions must not be described
  as an equivalence for unrestricted strategies reacting to additional public
  announcements without a separate proof.
* A tuple of marginal laws loses general correlation. Neither independent
  cards nor a product prior is silently assumed.
* An impossible public observation has no Bayes posterior. Total internal
  conditionals may have a fallback, but it must be proved unused under the
  sampled public law and must not be certified as a PBS at that observation.
* M02's full AOH policies have syntactic information states that need not be
  realizable. Restriction/extension must discharge `PBS-POLICY-DOMAIN` and
  retain off-policy deviations. An arbitrary compressed information model is
  not identified with full memory.
* Perfect-information degeneration needs enough public information to
  identify a history; individual knowledge alone does not imply this.
