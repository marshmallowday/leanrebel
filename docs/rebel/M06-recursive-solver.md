## Parent combinator

PBSComposedDepth uses the actual coupled parent recurrence with a computational
PBSChildSolve. Each current trunk constructs its factual joint PBS; the child
receives the existing joint mass-floor-scaled positive target. Public splicing
and counterfactual completion supply every live query, including zero-own-reach
queries. Numerical perturbation is applied to that same parent's values.

cfrDComposedOracle_accurate and cfrDComposedOracle_leafOptimal feed the existing
finite-T Nash theorem. pbsComposedDepthProfile computes a nonempty finite count
AFTER accounting for numerical and child errors, and transfers the rooted
all-deviation guarantee to the original PBS. Its chance-root translation retains
one administrative transition separately from the original cut length.

checkpoint. The parent combinator is not by itself an arbitrary-depth solver.

retain positive noise allocations and boundary examples rather than use a
supplied equilibrium or silently force all errors to zero.
