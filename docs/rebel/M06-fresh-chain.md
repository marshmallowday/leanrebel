CFRDFreshChain constructs a Nat-recursive sequence. Stage zero returns the
incumbent. Every successor calls the actual cfrDInformationContinuation on the
preceding result, including factual public child solving and zero-own-reach
completion. It does not accept an accuracy, equilibrium, support or envelope
certificate. Each call preserves the original unilateral reference prefix;
this is proved by induction using the implementation's preservation theorem.

The generic finite-family drift sum telescopes supported conditional changes
on that common reference law. Its constructed-chain consumer discharges the
reference-law premise, and the last child supplies local quality when its own
numerical tolerance is positive. This does not imply that previous invalid
numeric requests were accurate; no such claim is needed or made.

The new resolver privately draws a legal finite plan of the final computed
average. The full history law holds against every fixed unknown opponent,
including legal states outside model support and states with a missing model
belief. This inherits the existing coherent-plan resolver's semantics; it is
not the native per-CFR-iteration draw of the other carried-PBS implementation.
Intermediate computations are not shared random seeds or played stages.

For q+1 actual refreshes, the derived opponent envelope is

    final child loss(q) + sum_{n=0}^q measuredDrift(profile(n), profile(n+1)).

The final envelope has no caller-supplied local child optimality or reference-law
certificate. Nevertheless the drift sum remains a real-valued specification;
there is no assertion that it decreases with the child's Nash tolerance.
