## Quantities that must not be conflated

Let delta denote prediction error, ell strictly positive child tolerance, T the
positive outer iteration count, and alpha the positive mass floor derived from
one actual finite joint posterior. Let C_child denote that rooted game's
information-set finite-schedule coefficient, independent of its iteration count.

The child uses e = alpha * ell and N = floor((abs(C_child) / e)^2) + 1.
The Lean theorem factors its actual residual as C_child * sqrt(N) / N and proves
it is at most e. Alpha is not supplied as a hypothesis and need not have a
uniform positive lower bound over all possible learned beliefs. Very small
posteriors can therefore require very large child counts. No efficiency claim
or common fixed child-T correspondence follows from this construction.

The factual type marginal is at least alpha on its support. The reference-slice
mixture reconstructs the full factual joint law, allowing the weighted Nash
budget to become typewise ell. A type absent from the factual law is not
certified by dividing by zero: existing conditional best-response completion
handles zero-own-reach cases and preserves the cut reference law.

The parent guarantee keeps all three contributions:
(A0 + A1) * delta + (C0 + C1) / sqrt(T) + 2 * ell.
Setting delta to zero does not erase finite-T or child error. The strictly
positive child-tolerance premise is not replaced with an exact finite solve.
The exact comparison equilibrium only identifies the reference game value;
no child equilibrium or conditional-quality certificate is an input.

## Information and fallback review

The input fallback is a legal policy of the original reduced information model.
cfrDInformationFallback explicitly lifts it to full AOHs. This is not a claim
that arbitrary full-AOH policies can be projected to the reduced state.
All computed policies and quantified deviations use the full original AOH
interface. The public cut prefix selects the child; hidden worlds and opponent
observations are not local policy arguments. Complete baseline and unilateral
law equalities precede equilibrium transfer.

The model PBS is not equated to the unknown opponent's true hidden-state law.
No guarantee is made for each sampled pure plan separately. The private parent
iteration index is not resampled at each action or revealed to the opponent.
