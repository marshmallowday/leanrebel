# M06 zero-own-reach completion controls

The generic zero-own-reach completion, child adapters and repaired live-child
controls at `78107e72819214d73d81238c09d4423d922dad76` passed targeted run
`35477330791`, job `105988756019`. These are compiler results, not full M06

The new canonical HiddenTypes control changes the focal action at the legal
live history where that player previously chose true, although its baseline
always chooses false. Own reach is exactly zero. The local continuation value
against a fixed constant opponent changes from -2 to 0, while the complete
original-game law remains unchanged against EVERY fixed opposing policy.
Thus the completion is substantive, not a vacuous identity of policies.

The boundary control uses a different history: the focal player chose false,
but its opponent chose true. The original joint probability is zero, while
focal own reach is exactly one. The completion correctly leaves this focal
policy unchanged. Zero joint probability is not a substitute for zero own
reach in this construction.

These new example theorems require their own exact-source compiler and full
gates. They do not prove that every arbitrary completion is counterfactually
optimal. Constructing compatible optimal child completions and the recursive
PBS child solver remains a separate M06 obligation. No coverage row is promoted.
