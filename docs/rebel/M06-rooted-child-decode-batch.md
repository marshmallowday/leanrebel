## Integrated change and assumptions

child law inside an existing pbsRootProtocol. A public head of the form
some observations certifies that the administrative draw has occurred.
The analyst's total getD readout is used only on supported some states.
The posterior is mapped as a whole; it is not reset to the outer roots,
factorized into marginal beliefs, or identified with an unknown opponent's
factual posterior. The original outer cut still controls the information-local
AOH decoder. Equal outer depth is derived from the outer PublicBelief.

same decoded full continuation law from this child. Remaining fuel is unchanged:
the administrative chance unit is NOT added a second time. Zero fuel and terminal
histories are covered. The decode theorem transfers Nash at the same child law,
error and remaining horizon; it does not assume an individual private draw Nash.

algorithm and its actual finite private draw. Finite original histories/actions
supply finite rooted histories. Zero sum, nonnegative uniform payoff bound,
positive requested tolerance and the protocol-indexed noise contract are the
existing recursive Nash assumptions. The private law equality itself needs no
Nash/noise-accuracy assumption and holds for every execution fuel; the unknown
opponent remains outside the draw.

The existing parent's cfrDComposedChildTable chooses its actual factual live
child and requests child.law.positiveMassFloor * loss. The selected-entry theorem
preserves precisely that parameter, fallback, root encoding and protocol.
The parent's public-splice law is then connected to the decoded internal
recursive profile for arbitrary later fuel. No new safety/child-Nash certificate
is requested from the caller. The current algorithm and native update definitions
are unchanged.

A fresh original solver is separately recomputed on the decoded joint PBS.
Internal and fresh noise families, cut partitions, fallbacks, requested errors
and computed finite budgets may differ. At equal total remaining horizon their
computed selfplay scalar gap is bounded by internalError+freshError. Their
policies/kernels/conditional value vectors need not coincide. This comparison
does not identify algorithms across protocol-dependent noise.
